
Write a program that solves two problems and prints the answers, each on a separate line.

1. A painter wants to paint 8 sections of a fence, each in one of 2 colors. How many unique ways of painting are there? Each new section doubles the number of options, so the answer equals the number of colors raised to the power of the number of sections. Use the exponent operator `**`. The base, that is, the number of colors, goes to the left of it, and the exponent, that is, the number of sections, goes to the right. The order of operands matters, because `a ** b` and `b ** a` give different results.
2. You and two friends (3 people in total) bought 9 cakes and want to share them equally. How many does each person get? Use the division operator `/`.
