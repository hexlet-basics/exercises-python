You and 4 friends (5 people in total) ordered 2 pizzas at 300 each and 5 drinks at 40 each. The bill has to be split equally.

Write a one-line program with `print()`, placing the parentheses so that the total is calculated first and only then divided among everyone:

```text
2 * 300 + 5 * 40 / 5  =  640.0  ← without parentheses the division applies only to the drinks
```

Expected output: `160.0`.
